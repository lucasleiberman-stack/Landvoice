Longevity

No One Has Been Doing This Longer.

Landvoice was founded in 1991 - before the modern MLS, before online listing portals, before the iPhone, and before every other seller data provider in this industry existed. The first FSBO lists were built by hand from newspaper classifieds and faxed to agents. Delivery moved to email, then CD-ROM, then to the cloud platform agents use today.

Thirty-five years later, we're still doing the same job: finding homeowners who need to sell now and verifying how to reach them.

That history isn't nostalgia - it's the entire reason our data is more accurate.
