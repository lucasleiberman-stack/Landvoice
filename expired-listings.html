Expired listings

Why Expired Listings Are the Fastest Path to More Listings

Expired leads are homeowners who have already decided to move. The majority of expireds make a decision to relist their home within the first thirty days.

What Are Expired, Canceled, and Withdrawn Listings?

Expired, Canceled, and Withdrawn Listings are homes that came off the market without selling. Terminology varies between MLSs, so the same situation can carry different labels in different markets. For prospecting purposes the practical difference is small, because in every one of these cases a homeowner tried to sell and was unsuccessful.

What Landvoice Expired Data Includes

Homeowner information including name, phone numbers, email, and social media links. All phone numbers are scrubbed against the National and State Do Not Call Registry. Landvoice Expired Data also includes current status, price, days on market, as well as other relevant homeowner information.

  • Phone numbers
  • Email
  • Social media links
  • Current status
  • Price
  • Days on market

Why Listings Expire and What to Say About It

The failure of the previous agent provides an opportunity for you to have an intelligent conversation with the homeowner to relist the home.

  1. The home didn’t show well.
  2. The home wasn’t priced effectively.
  3. The previous agent didn’t market the property effectively.
